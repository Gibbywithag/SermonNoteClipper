================================================================
  SERMON CLIPPER  —  READ ME FIRST
================================================================

SET UP (one time):

  1) Double-click:   Install Sermon Clipper.command

  2) If your Mac warns that it "cannot verify the developer"
     or mentions malware — this is NORMAL and SAFE. It only
     means the app didn't come from the App Store.

        →  RIGHT-CLICK the file, choose "Open", then "Open".

     (On newer Macs, if you don't see an "Open" option:
        Apple menu  →  System Settings  →  Privacy & Security
        →  scroll down  →  click "Open Anyway".)

  3) Follow the prompts. The very first setup takes about
     10-20 minutes (it installs things and builds the app).
     You only do this once.

----------------------------------------------------------------

EVERY DAY AFTER:

  Just double-click:   Sermon Clipper.app
  (Tip: drag it onto your Dock for one-click access.)
